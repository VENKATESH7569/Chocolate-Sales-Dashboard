# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Dashboard

## Overview

The Chocolate Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, geographic distribution, and salesperson effectiveness. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights through dynamic visualizations, KPI tracking, and interactive filtering.

This project demonstrates data modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard design, and business intelligence reporting using Power BI.

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to help stakeholders:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Evaluate salesperson performance
* Compare geographic sales distribution
* Track profitability and shipment metrics
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Key Insights

* Organic Choco Syrup emerged as one of the highest revenue-generating products.
* Revenue showed strong seasonal patterns with noticeable peaks during specific months.
* Top-performing salespersons contributed significantly to overall revenue growth.
* Geographic analysis revealed differences in revenue contribution across regions.
* Profit margins remained consistently strong across major product categories.
